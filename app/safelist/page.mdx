## Email & Web

- While our servers are hosted on Amazon web services, Stadium's transactional emails are triggered through Mandrill (also known as Mailchimp Transactional).
- **STEP 1:** Add IP addresses to safelist: Mandrill continually adds new IP addresses in their shared pool. However, checking the SPF record of mandrill (spf.mandrillapp.com), we can see the list of their delivery IPs that we can use to update safelists.
- **STEP 2:** Add domains to safelist: Please make sure you configure the following hosts as trusted senders on your email server and firewall so that mails from Stadium and its affiliated brands aren't rejected.
- **STEP 3:** Make sure the email addresses below are not routed to spam or graymail. If you don't do this, your treat recipients may not receive their invitation, order confirmation, and password reset emails.
- **STEP 4:** Be sure to safelist the following hosts in your firewall for outbound TCP connections over ports 80 and 443 (for HTTP and HTTPS, respectively).
